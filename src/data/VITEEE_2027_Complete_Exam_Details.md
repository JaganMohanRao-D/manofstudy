# VITEEE 2027: Complete Exam Details, Important Dates, Eligibility, Pattern, Campuses & Courses

VITEEE 2027 (VIT Engineering Entrance Examination) is the official
entrance examination for admission to undergraduate B.Tech. programmes
offered across the VIT Group of Institutions. The exam is expected to be
conducted from April 27 to May 2, 2027, in computer-based test mode at
designated centres across India and abroad. 

Applications for VITEEE 2027 opened on October 20, 2026, and the last
date to apply is March 31, 2027.

## What Is VITEEE 2027?

VITEEE is conducted by the Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT) for
admission to its B.Tech. programmes. It is mandatory for Indian
candidates seeking admission to B.Tech. courses at VIT; JEE Main or SAT
scores are not considered for B.Tech. admission.

The examination is a Computer-Based Test (CBT) conducted in English.
Each candidate can appear for VITEEE 2027 only once; any violation may
lead to cancellation of the application and admission.

## VITEEE 2027 Exam Pattern

The VITEEE 2027 question paper will contain 100 multiple-choice
questions to be completed in 2 hours. \[1\]

### Marking Scheme

-   Correct answer: +4 marks
-   Wrong answer: -1 mark
-   Unanswered or marked for review: 0 marks \[1\]

### Subject-Wise Question Distribution

  Subject                   Number of Questions
  ----------------------- ---------------------
  Mathematics / Biology                40 \[1\]
  Physics                              25 \[1\]
  Chemistry                            25 \[1\]
  Aptitude                              5 \[1\]
  English                               5 \[1\]
  **Total**                             **100**

### MPCEA and BPCEA Options

Candidates can choose one of the following subject combinations:

  Option   Subjects
  -------- ----------------------------------------------------------
  MPCEA    Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, English, Aptitude \[1\]
  BPCEA    Biology, Physics, Chemistry, English, Aptitude \[1\]

Students opting for MPCEA are eligible for all B.Tech. programmes,
subject to meeting the eligibility requirements. Students opting for
BPCEA are eligible only for selected biology-related programmes, such as
Biotechnology, Bioengineering, Bioinformatics, Health Sciences and
Technology, and certain health-informatics or education-technology
programmes. \[1\]

### Section-Wise Time Allocation

  Section                 Duration
  ----------------------- ------------------
  Mathematics / Biology   50 minutes \[1\]
  Physics                 30 minutes \[1\]
  Chemistry               30 minutes \[1\]
  English and Aptitude    10 minutes \[1\]
  **Total**               **2 hours**

## VITEEE 2027 Application Fee

The application fee for VITEEE 2027 is:

-   Rs. 1,450 for test centres in India \[1\]
-   USD 90 equivalent in INR for test centres abroad \[1\]

The fee can be paid through Net Banking, Credit Card, or Debit Card. The
application and subsequent counselling fee is non-refundable. \[1\]

## VITEEE 2027 Eligibility Criteria

### Nationality

Applicants must be:

-   Resident Indian Nationals, or
-   Non-Resident Indians (NRIs), or
-   OCI Cardholders with a residential address in India. \[1\]

NRI and foreign applicants may apply through the relevant NRI or
International application route as specified by VIT. \[1\]

### Qualifying Examination

Candidates must have passed Class XII / HSC / Intermediate in 2025 or
2026, or must be appearing for the examination in 2027. Candidates who
passed Class XII in 2024 or earlier are not eligible for VITEEE 2027.
\[1\]

The qualifying examination must be completed through regular, full-time,
and formal education, with English as one of the subjects. \[1\]

### Minimum Marks Required

For candidates applying through the PCM route:

-   Minimum 60% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics
-   Minimum 50% in Mathematics \[1\]

For candidates applying through the PCB route:

-   Minimum 60% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology
-   Minimum 50% in Biology \[1\]

For candidates belonging to SC/ST categories, or from Jammu & Kashmir,
Ladakh, and the North-Eastern states, the minimum aggregate requirement
in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology is 50%. Relevant
community or nativity certificates must be produced during counselling.
\[1\]

## VITEEE 2027 Application Process

Candidates can apply online through the official portal. The major steps
are: \[1\]

1.  Sign up using a valid email address and active mobile number.
2.  Verify the registered mobile number using the OTP.
3.  Log in and accept the declaration.
4.  Fill in personal, communication, and educational details.
5.  Select the preferred subject combination: MPCEA or BPCEA.
6.  Upload the Class 10 mark sheet and required documents.
7.  Capture a live photograph and upload a signature.
8.  Pay the application fee online.
9.  Download and save the completed application form.

Candidates do not need to send a hard copy of the application form to
VIT. \[1\]

### Documents Required

Applicants should keep the following documents ready while applying:

-   Class 10 mark sheet
-   Aadhaar Card
-   Passport
-   Voter ID Card

-   Ration Card or Family Card
-   Address, Domicile, or Residence Certificate
-   Bank Passbook or Bank Statement showing the correspondence address
-   School ID Card with photograph and address, where applicable \[1\]

## VITEEE 2027 Counselling Process

Admission to VIT B.Tech. programmes is offered only through the VITEEE
2027 rank-based online counselling process. Candidates can choose their
preferred campus, programme, and fee category during counselling,
subject to rank and seat availability. \[1\]

Key points about counselling:

-   Candidates must secure a valid VITEEE rank.
-   Campus and programme preferences are selected during counselling,
    not during the application process. \[1\]
-   Seat allotment depends on VITEEE rank, eligibility, and available
    seats.
-   Eligibility is verified after tuition-fee payment within the
    prescribed deadline.
-   Being called for counselling does not guarantee admission. \[1\]
-   After allotment, VIT does not offer branch change, campus change, or
    category upgradation. \[1\]

## Preparation Tips for VITEEE 2027

-   Focus equally on Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics or Biology, as
    these sections carry the maximum weightage.
-   Practise previous-year-style multiple-choice questions and timed
    mock tests.
-   Strengthen fundamental concepts rather than relying only on formula
    memorisation.
-   Pay attention to English grammar, vocabulary, comprehension, and
    logical aptitude.
-   Since incorrect answers carry negative marking, avoid blind
    guessing.
-   Attempt the official mock test expected to be enabled in March 2027.
    \[1\]

## Important Points to Remember

-   VITEEE 2027 is mandatory for Indian candidates seeking B.Tech.
    admission at VIT. \[1\]
-   There is no management quota for B.Tech. admission at VIT. \[1\]
-   There is no lateral-entry admission for diploma holders into the
    B.Tech. programme. \[1\]
-   There is no specific age limit, but candidates must meet the Class
    XII passing-year requirement. \[1\]
-   Candidates should apply only through the official websites:
    www.vit.ac.in or https://viteee.vit.ac.in/, as VIT has warned
    applicants about fake websites. \[1\]
